# Renaissance visual study

These public-domain reproductions are included only in the local Shiftora visual-study branch. They are historical editorial analogies, not evidence of the engagements presented beside them. No production use has been approved.

Each selected JPEG has 960px and 1800px WebP derivatives plus a native-width WebP candidate for responsive delivery. All public presentation is monochrome through CSS; source colour is retained in the files.

## Selected set

- `arrival-ambassadors.jpg` — Vittore Carpaccio, *Arrival of the English Ambassadors*, c. 1495–1500. Local derivative: resized to 2600 × 1247, metadata stripped, JPEG quality 86. Source: [Wikimedia Commons public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Accademia_-_Arrivo_degli_ambasciatori_inglesi_presso_il_re_di_Bretagna_di_Vittore_Carpaccio.jpg). Official object record: [Gallerie dell’Accademia](https://www.gallerieaccademia.it/en/arrival-ambassadors). Acquired 2026-09-04.
- `arrival-evidence.jpg`, `arrival-evidence-960.webp`, `arrival-evidence-1800.webp`, and `arrival-evidence-2600.webp` — art-directed homepage derivatives of `arrival-ambassadors.jpg`; upper 2600 × 960 crop removes the later black infill at the lower edge while retaining the diplomatic assembly and civic architecture. JPEG quality 86; WebP quality 82; metadata stripped.
- `ideal-city.jpg` — Central Italian painter, *The Ideal City (Urbino)*, c. 1480–1490. Local derivative: 1920 × 551 JPEG. Source: [Wikimedia Commons public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ideal_City_-_Urbino.jpg). Official object record: [Galleria Nazionale delle Marche](https://gndm.it/en/opere/citta-ideale/). Acquired 2026-09-03.
- `pacioli.jpg` — attributed to Jacopo de’ Barbari, *Portrait of Luca Pacioli and a Young Man*, c. 1495–1500. Local derivative: resized to 2200 × 1833, metadata stripped, JPEG quality 86. Source: [Wikimedia Commons public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jacopo_de%27_Barbari_(attributed_to)_Portrait_of_Luca_Pacioli_(1445_1517)_with_a_student_(Guidobaldo_da_Montefeltro)_(2).jpg). Acquired 2026-09-04.
- `athens.jpg` — Raphael, *The School of Athens*, 1509–1511. Local derivative: 1920 × 1339 JPEG. Source: [Wikimedia Commons public-domain record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raphael_School_of_Athens.jpg). Official object record: [Vatican Museums](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/stanze-di-raffaello/stanza-della-segnatura/scuola-di-atene.html). Acquired 2026-09-03.
